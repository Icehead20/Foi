Armchair Neo

Armchair Neo are Natura Oceans Lovcraimp that crawl along the shores of the Carala Region in search of food and nesting.

Appearance

Armchair Neo are black, flat, elongated creatures covered by a smooth shell decorated with purple spots. They have sharp pink mouths and eyes, as well as four short pink legs. They also possess two large purple limbs that end in black claws with yellow and pink gradients.

Description

Armchair Neo are capable of inhabiting both aquatic and terrestrial environments, but cannot survive for extended periods in either one exclusively. They will eventually drown if kept underwater for too long, while prolonged exposure to dry environments causes them to become dehydrated. As a result, they primarily inhabit shorelines, rarely venturing too far underwater or onto dry land.

Although they can consume water through their mouths, it passes through their bodies without being processed or absorbed. They can only properly absorb water through the skin on their undersides.

They establish their nests by burrowing into wet sand, usually not far from the shoreline. Individuals tend to return to the general area where they were born to establish their own nests. They are particularly precise about how deep they burrow, consistently remaining within a specific underground depth range that they do not exceed.

Armchair Neo lay large numbers of eggs in underground chambers located below their usual burrowing depth. These eggs are small, hard, and resemble pebbles. They are kept warm by the surrounding sand, while the progenitor usually remains buried directly above them, acting as a protective cover.

Due to the size and structure of their claws, Armchair Neo cannot walk sideways and are limited to moving forwards and backwards. This forces them to make wide turns when changing direction. When burrowing, they rapidly shake their bodies above the sand to work their way underground.

Their large claws also make them relatively cumbersome. They usually drag them along the ground and rarely use them for anything other than emergencies or combat. Their primary means of fighting is biting. When they do use their claws offensively, they rely on their weight and swing them against their targets with considerable force, much like hammers. The claws themselves contain very little soft tissue and consist mostly of hard shell, with limited opening and closing movement.

Armchair Neo are herbivores, feeding on small roots and algae found underwater. They also use their claws to smash coconuts and consume their contents. A large proportion of the coconuts that wash ashore from Coconut Island to other parts of Carala are eaten by them. This is believed to be one of the reasons coconut trees are considerably less common in other parts of the region than on the island itself.

During mating season, Armchair Neo engage in fights with one another, biting and scratching with their legs to attack their opponents. These confrontations are usually brief and rarely result in serious injuries. Victories are thought to improve an individual's chances of being selected by females for mating. However, females sometimes choose the losers instead, and it remains unknown whether they follow any particular criteria when selecting the losers over the winners.

The meat found on their undersides is edible and has experienced several changes in popularity over time. It was once considered a lower-class food, but eventually became extremely popular in distant regions, leading to large-scale exports. Its popularity has since stabilized, and it is now regarded as an ordinary food source.

Armchair Neo are preyed upon by Stunyer and other Bird species, which catch them and carry them high into the air before dropping them to break their shells and access their meat. They are also regularly hunted by other Oceans species with sufficiently strong teeth, as well as certain S/A CosiPlants species.

Despite being preyed upon by numerous creatures, Armchair Neo populations remain stable. Their exceptionally high egg production allows their numbers to recover and expand throughout Carala, preventing the species from reaching dangerously low population levels.

The origin of their name is a subject of debate. One explanation suggests that their elongated bodies and large claws give them the appearance of small armchairs. Another theory proposes that the name is a pun referring to their large limbs as "arms" resting or “sitting” on the sand. The "Neo" part of the name is more widely understood to reference the bright colors of their features, which contrast with their black shells and resemble neon lights.
